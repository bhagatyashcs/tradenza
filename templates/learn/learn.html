The 5 Golden Rules of Capital Preservation

Trading is a game of defense first. Offense takes care of itself.
1. The 2% Capital Risk Limit Non-Negotiable

Never risk more than 1.5% to 2.0% of your total portfolio equity on any single trade. A string of 5 consecutive losses at 2% risk equals a 10% drawdown, which is recoverable in days. The same streak at 10% risk requires a +100% gain just to break even.

2. Stop Loss Anchoring Hard Rule

Never enter a trade without an anchored Stop Loss placed in the market. Moving a stop loss further away once price approaches it is the #1 cause of catastrophic account blowups. Accept predefined risk with detachment.

3. Asymmetric Reward-to-Risk (≥ 1:1.5R) Mathematical Edge

Only take setups offering at least 1.5x to 2x reward relative to your risk distance. With a 1:2 R:R ratio, you can be wrong 60% of the time and still compound profits consistently.

4. The 15-Minute Cooldown Protocol Tilt Firebreak

After closing a losing trade, your brain enters an instinctive fight-or-flight state seeking immediate revenge. Tradenza enforces a 15-minute timeout. Step away from the monitor, hydrate, and breathe before analyzing the next opportunity.

5. Pre-Flight Execution Gate Checklist Audit

Before clicking "Buy" or "Sell", verify all 5 pre-flight checklist gates in your Tradenza Trade Cockpit: Symbol, Stop Loss, Target, Strategy tag, and Emotional check. If one is missing, you do not have a trade—you have a gamble.

🚀 Breakout Setup

Momentum

Trading expansion after price consolidates within a tight range against key support or resistance.

Trigger: High-volume candle closing outside the consolidation range.
Stop Loss: Back inside the range, below the breakout candle low.
Target: Measured move of previous consolidation range (1:2 R).

📈 Pullback / Retest

Trend Continuation

Entering an established trend when price temporarily retraces to key dynamic support (20 EMA, VWAP, or broken level).

Trigger: Reversal rejection candle (hammer, engulfing) at retest level.
Stop Loss: Just beyond the retest swing point or moving average.
Target: Retest of recent swing high with runner target at 1:2.5R.

🔄 Mean Reversion / Reversal

Counter-Trend

Fading extended momentum when buyers or sellers exhaust their liquidity into multi-timeframe extremes.

Trigger: RSI divergence + failed higher high (liquidity sweep).
Stop Loss: Above the sweep extreme wick.
Target: Mean baseline (VWAP or 50% retracement level).

Mastering the Trader's Mindset

Trading is 20% technical strategy and 80% emotional governance.
Defeating Revenge Trading

The market cannot be punished. When you take a loss, your account took a mathematical hit, but your identity is intact. Treat every loss like a business expense (e.g. paying rent).

Eliminating FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)

The market will print opportunities every minute of every trading day for the rest of your life. Chasing an extended green candle is buying someone else's exit liquidity. Wait for the retest.

Letting Winners Run

If you take 1.5R or 2R on winners, you do not need a 70% win rate. When in profit, trail your stop loss to break-even + buffer, then leave the chart to prevent premature closure out of anxiety.
