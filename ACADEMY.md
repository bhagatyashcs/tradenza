# Tradenza Academy Curriculum Specification

## 1. Academy Mission & Learning Model

The **Tradenza Academy** is designed around **Just-In-Time Behavioral Learning**. Unlike static trading courses that overwhelm beginners with theory, Tradenza Academy delivers targeted educational lessons triggered dynamically when a trader exhibits specific cognitive leaks or psychological biases.

```text
Trade Execution Logged
         │
         ▼
Behavior & Insight Engines Detect Pattern
         │
         ▼
Aura AI Recommends Targeted Academy Module
         │
         ▼
Trader Completes 5-Minute Practical Lesson & Daily Mission
```

## 3. Detailed Lesson Outlines

### Module 1: Eliminating Revenge Trading (`lesson_emotions_and_revenge_trading`)
- **The Psychology**: Experiencing a financial loss triggers an acute fight-or-flight response. The subconscious brain perceives the loss as a threat and demands immediate restitution, causing traders to re-enter impulsively with larger position sizes.
- **The Protocol**:
  1. *Hard Disconnect*: Step away from the screens for a minimum of 20 to 30 minutes following any stop-out.
  2. *Reset Journal*: Record pre-entry emotion in Tradenza before re-entering any subsequent position.
  3. *Size Ceiling*: Lock position size to equal or smaller than the baseline trade.

### Module 2: The Art of Holding Winners (`lesson_holding_winners_to_target`)
- **The Psychology**: The pain of a winning trade turning into a loss exceeds the joy of reaching a full target ("Prospect Theory"). As a result, traders bank micro-profits while allowing losers to run to full stops.
- **The Protocol**:
  1. *Define Invalidation Before Entry*: Establish target levels grounded in market structure, not emotional profit dollar amounts.
  2. *Trailing Rules*: Move stop-loss to breakeven only after reaching 1:1 R:R, and use structure-based trailing stops.
  3. *Partial Exits*: Take 50% at target 1 to de-risk, letting the runner reach target 2.

### Module 3: Mathematical Risk & Fractional Sizing (`lesson_position_sizing_mastery`)
- **The Formula**:
  $$\text{Position Size} = \frac{\text{Account Capital} \times \text{Risk \%}}{\text{Entry Price} - \text{Stop Loss Price}}$$
- **Rule of 2%**: Never risk more than 2.0% of total liquid capital on any single speculative thesis.
- **R:R Expectancy**: Ensure every setup features a mathematical expectancy of at least $1.5 \times \text{Risk}$.

## 4. Assessment & Completion Mechanics

- **Interactive Checkpoints**: 3-question practical scenario check at the end of each module.
- **Genome Feedback**: When a trader completes a lesson and demonstrates 10 subsequent trades without the targeted bias, their **Learning Rate** and **Discipline** scores receive an automatic bonus (+15 pts).
